# Supersurf
![Supersurf logo](icons/icon-2-256.png)
Surf the web at the speed of sound.

Supersurf is a fully-featured browser extension that makes surfing the web without a keyboard possible and easy. Hold down the keybind and simply say to open Rick Astley on YouTube, pin your tabs, close them, and much more.

It works with Fish Audio and Google Gemini to read and call browser functionality.

Made by four UW-Madison Students for Madhacks 2025.
